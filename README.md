## Project description  

This project is dedicated to creating interactive and deep analytics for a global premium delicatessen retailer. Based on a real-world dataset, an analytical solution was developed in Power BI Desktop, which allows sales directors, logistics managers, and HR specialists to make informed decisions to increase profitability and delivery efficiency.  

## Purpose and key objectives  
  
**Main goal:** To provide company management with a tool to understand order distribution, employee performance, and the effectiveness of interaction with logistics partners.  
  
**Key business questions:**  
  
- What are the company's financial results and how do they change over time?  
- Which customers are the most loyal and profitable (TOP customers)?  
- How efficiently do our logistics partners (Shippers) work? Do they meet delivery deadlines?  
- What are the results and ratings of individual sales managers? Who provides too big discounts?   
  
## Methodology and implementation stages  
  
The project was implemented using the classic Business Intelligence (BI) methodology:   

**Stage 1. ETL and data preparation**    
  
- **Loading and cleaning (Power Query):** Data was loaded from CSV and TXT files, cleaned, typed, and corrected table names for business users.  
   
  ![Loading and cleaning](https://github.com/isachenko-andrii/Order-and-logistics-analysis/blob/main/img/ola_1.png)  

 - **Calendar (DimDate):** A separate Date table has been created using a DAX script to ensure that time intelligence works correctly. It is marked as a Date table.  
  
![DimDate_Calendar](https://github.com/isachenko-andrii/Order-and-logistics-analysis/blob/main/img/ola_2.png)  
  
**Stage 2. Data Modeling**  
  
**Star Architecture:** An optimal data model is designed, where the central fact tables (Orders, Order_details) are connected to the dimension tables (Customers, Employees, Shippers, Products, Categories, Calendar).

## Key findings  
  
**1. Sales and geographic concentration**  
  
**Top markets:** The main revenue generators are USA ($245.6k) and Germany ($230.3k). Together with Austria ($128k), they account for over 47% of all global sales.  
**Category drivers:** The most popular are Beverages ($267.9k) and Dairy Products ($234.5k). The lowest revenue is generated by Grains & Cereals ($95.7k).  
  
**2. Sales team effectiveness (Managers)**  
    
**Absolute leader:** Margaret Peacock demonstrates the best results — $232.9k (156 orders) with a moderate average discount of 6.9%.  
**The Problem of Ineffective Discounts:** Manager Robert King has the highest average discount rate — 11.8% (almost double the company average), but his sales are only $124.6k.  
**Outsiders:** Steven Buchanan has the lowest results — just $68.8k for 42 orders.  
  
**3. Logistics Analysis (Carriers)**  
  
**Quality Leader:** The most reliable partner is Federal Shipping with a lateness rate of just 3.53% (9 latenesses out of 255 orders).  
**The busiest partner:** United Package fulfills the most orders (326), but has the highest lateness rate — 4.91%.  
  
## Strategic proposals  
  
**1. Optimization of commercial policy (Prices and Discounts)**  
  
**Discount control:** Set a hard limit on the maximum discount (cap) at 8% for managers. The Robert King case proves that large discounts do not guarantee leadership in sales, but only “burn” the company’s margin.  
**Incentivizing outsiders:** Transfer the client base of Steven Buchanan and other weaker managers under the supervision of leaders (Margaret Peacock, Janet Leverling) to analyze sales style and conduct internal coaching.  
  
**2. Restructuring logistics contracts**  
  
**Traffic redistribution:** Partially redirect orders from United Package (4.91% late) in favor of Federal Shipping (3.53% late) for priority and expensive orders.  
**Penalties:** Implement financial penalties in the SLA (service level agreement) with carriers for exceeding the 4% delay threshold.  
  
**3. Focus on high-margin markets**  
  
**Local campaigns:** Launch targeted marketing activities in the US and Germany markets to promote the Beverages and Dairy Products categories, as these markets have the highest purchasing power and the fastest return on investment (ROI).
